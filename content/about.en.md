Welcome to **XinqAI** (欣淇AI网 in Chinese), a hands-on blog about **AI tools, free resources and practical tutorials**.

The Chinese edition of this site has been publishing since 2026 for readers in mainland China. This English edition focuses on what English-language coverage tends to miss: **the fast-moving Chinese AI scene**. New models from DeepSeek, Moonshot (Kimi), MiniMax, Zhipu (GLM), StepFun and Alibaba (Qwen) often ship with Chinese-only announcements, pricing pages and sign-up flows. We read the originals, test the products, and explain them in plain English:

- **What actually changed**: specs, benchmarks (with a note on who ran them), and context windows.
- **What it costs**: API pricing, free tiers and subscription plans, compared side by side.
- **How to use it from outside China**: which entry points work, and what to watch out for.

Every article ends with a fact-check note saying where the facts came from and when they were checked.
