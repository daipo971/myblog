Welcome to **XinqAI** (欣淇AI网 in Chinese), an independent technology platform dedicated to **hands-on AI tool evaluation, developer productivity workflows, and international tech guides**.

This site focuses on bridging the gap between developers worldwide and the fast-moving AI scene:

- **In-Depth Benchmarking**: DeepSeek, Moonshot (Kimi), MiniMax, Zhipu (GLM), StepFun, and Alibaba (Qwen), evaluated alongside international leaders like Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini.
- **Developer Productivity**: Real-world developer tools, API cost optimization, terminal automation, and cross-border workflows.
- **Transparent Methodology**: Every review is based on verified hands-on testing, reproducible environments, and official documentation citations.
